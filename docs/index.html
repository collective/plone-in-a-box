Plone in a Box™

Your own Plone 6 website on a cloud server, without needing a command line.

  1. Pick a provider: Linode, DigitalOcean, AWS or Hetzner.
  2. Choose a password for your site's admin account.
  3. Click Create. About 10 minutes later, your server's address shows your new Plone site.
Deploy on Linode Launch on AWS DigitalOcean or Hetzner

New to all of them? Choose Linode: a form, nothing to paste, and among the lowest prices. Already have an account somewhere? Use that one. Prefer a European provider? Hetzner.

You need an account with the provider and a way to pay. A small server costs a few cents an hour, paid to the provider. Nothing to install on your computer.

No need to decide everything now

Everything else can be added once your site is running:

  • your domain name
  • email, so your site can send password resets and form results
  • offsite backups, encrypted copies in cloud storage
  • notifications on your phone or in your team chat
  • logs & graphs of your server and site
  • add-ons and a theme
  • a control panel: how your site is doing, on your phone, with buttons to back up now and more (or tick Control panel when you create the server)

Linode (Akamai)

  1. Log in to Cloud Manager, open the Plone in a Box StackScript, and click Deploy New Linode.
  2. Fill in the Plone admin password (at least 12 characters).
  3. Choose Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, a region, a 1 GB Nanode and No firewall, set a root password, and click Create Linode.

Then open the Linode's IP address in a browser. More detail: setup guide.

Amazon AWS

  1. Sign in to the AWS console, then click Launch on AWS.
  2. Fill in the AdminPassword (at least 12 characters), and click Create stack.
  3. When the stack says CREATE_COMPLETE, open the SiteURL on its Outputs tab.

The default t3.small server costs more than the other providers' 1 GB ones. More detail: setup guide.

DigitalOcean and Hetzner

Create an Ubuntu 26.04 server with 1 GB of memory or more, and paste these lines into Additional Options → Startup scripts (DigitalOcean) or Cloud config (Hetzner). Replace CHANGE-ME with your admin password (at least 12 characters), keeping the quotes:

#!/bin/bash
export ADMIN_PASSWORD='CHANGE-ME'
curl -fsSL --retry 10 --retry-all-errors -o /root/plone-in-a-box.sh https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tkimnguyen/plone-in-a-box/2.27/src/StackScript && bash /root/plone-in-a-box.sh

Then open the server's IP address in a browser. More detail: DigitalOcean, Hetzner.

Make it yours

Know what the site is for? Fill in just these fields when you create the server:

Just trying PloneNothing more: your site opens on a welcome page that shows you where to start.
A site for your organizationDomain name, the Email fields and a Notification URL; then offsite backups.
An intranetBackend image and Frontend image: kitconcept's intranet.
Letting others try your siteA demo login, with the roles you choose.
Training or a workshopA server per person, each with a demo login, or Terraform for many at once; tick Control panel to give the demo login a new password after each session.
Showing off your add-onA Try It page and button for your README (how).
Full controlTerraform, your own images, and every setting in the setup guide.
